
  ══ A. WINDOW LENGTH ══
  The OR window is HARDCODED at 30 min in computeFeatures (engine.ts OPEN_RANGE_MIN); the spec's
  opening_range `minutes` is DECORATIVE — never plumbed through. So 15/30/45/60 are identical and a
  real window sweep needs an engine change (parameterize OPEN_RANGE_MIN through simulateSession).
  The productive lever is the WIDTH that the (any) window produces — tested below.

## What “bounded retune” means

A bounded retune is one dark, reversible A/B comparison—not a live rewrite and not a parameter search.

- The existing channel is the unchanged control.
- One variable receives one predeclared alternative. Size, entry, exit, and manager cannot move together.
- Both arms score the same future logical opportunities, so multiple fills do not masquerade as more evidence.
- The first review occurs after at least 5 new independent sessions and 10 scored logical outcomes. Collection continues when those counts disagree or uncertainty remains wide.
- A candidate must improve the typical session and at least 60% of paired sessions, avoid worse downside and material new displacement, and remain useful after removing its largest winner.
- A failed arm is removed; the baseline collector continues unchanged.

The 42 candidates are a research queue, not 42 proposed production changes:

- 16 priority B: adequate evidence, but mixed behavior or a smaller cohort makes the experiment less urgent.
- 8 priority C: enough evidence to form an inference, but not enough to spend the first research capacity on them.

All 42 source cohorts are historical-unstamped. Their history nominates the experiment; only the new prospective versioned cohort can validate the retune.

# Institutional channel research program

SEVE now treats the sealed roster as four research books. These labels explain why a channel is being studied; they do not change the worker, account route, order authority, or sealed execution posture.

| Book | Channels | Operating question |
|---|---:|---|
| Provisional core | 2 | Does the channel continue to earn control status under clean, current evidence? |
| Live paper experiments | 5 | Does one frozen change beat its control on paired logical opportunities? |
| Shadow investigations | 12 | Is there unique, repeatable opportunity worth a future paper test? |
| Archive | 5 | Is there a specific unanswered question strong enough to justify reopening collection? |

The nightly learning runner regenerates the assignments, experiment clocks, compact channel metrics, and no more than three operator decisions. It writes local JSON and Markdown artifacts and enriches the dashboard brief bundle. Publication remains a separate bounded step, and published briefs remain read-only.

## Decision discipline

1. A channel never becomes permanent core. It must keep earning the label.
2. A live experiment changes one variable and keeps entry, exit, manager, size, route, and collision policy fixed unless that item is the named variable.
3. The normal score point is five independent sessions and ten logical opportunities. Safety or evidence-integrity failures can stop a test earlier.
4. Typical session, downside, opportunity capture, paired improvement, displacement, and uniqueness matter more than total profit or raw win rate.
5. The nightly process can nominate decisions but cannot activate, resize, retune, promote, pause, route, or trade a channel.

## Dashboard hierarchy

- Research opens with the four-book board and a maximum-three decision inbox.
- Selecting a channel reveals its single current question and evidence clock.
- The Channel Inspector repeats the same research posture beside the sealed runtime posture so the two cannot be confused.
- Mobile shows the book identity on the collapsed channel row and the full research card inside the channel.
- Methods, provenance, and paired comparisons stay behind progressive disclosure.
